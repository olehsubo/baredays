Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to subotinoleg0@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days.

How do the colours work?

You log each day as "No sugar" or "I had sugar". A sugar day is red. Your first clean day is yellow, and from the second clean day in a row it turns green. A day you did not log breaks the run, so log it if you remember it: tap the day in Stats.

I forgot to log a day

Open Stats and tap the day. You can change the last 7 days for free; with the trial or Pro you can change any past day.

How do I keep my days when I change or reset my phone?

Turn on backup: Stats, then Settings, then Continue with Apple. On the new phone, sign in the same way and your days come back. Without backup, your days live only on your iPhone.

How do I delete my backup account?

Stats, then Settings, then Delete backup account. Your account and backed-up days are deleted from our server right away. The days on your iPhone stay.

I bought Pro but it is not unlocked

Open Stats, then Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Bare Days in the iPhone Settings app, then pick a reminder time in Stats, then Settings. No reminder is sent on a day you have already logged.
