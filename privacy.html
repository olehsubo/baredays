Bare Days

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Bare Days works without an account. The days you log stay on your iPhone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you choose to turn on backup, your logged days are stored on our server so you can get them back on a new phone, and you can delete that backup at any time inside the app.

Who we are

Bare Days is made by Oleh Subotin (individual entrepreneur, Ukraine). Contact: subotinoleg0@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you do in the app is saved on your iPhone only, unless you turn on backup (below):

  • The days you log: the date, whether it was a "no sugar" or a "sugar" day, and when you last changed it.
  • Your settings: the reminder time, the date you first opened the app, and whether you have started the trial or bought Pro.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. We do not run a notification server.

Optional backup with Sign in with Apple

If you tap Continue with Apple in Settings, we create a backup account for you. We store:

  • An account identifier that Apple gives to Bare Days (a random code that is unique to this app). We do not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • The days you log: the date, "no sugar" or "sugar", when you changed the day, and when you cleared a day.

We use this data only to back up and sync your days between your devices. Backups are stored with our hosting provider Supabase, in their Central EU (Frankfurt, Germany) data centre. Like any server, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs (for example IP addresses) to run and secure the service; we do not use them for anything else.

Purchases

The 14-day trial and Pro are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple which items you own (and when the trial started) so it can unlock features; this information is kept on your iPhone.

What we do not collect

We do not use analytics or advertising tools, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising. The app does not access your location, contacts, photos or the Health app.

Who else handles your data

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, purchases and notifications, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Supabase: stores the optional backup on our behalf. Supabase acts as our data processor and may use the data only to provide the service to us, with protection equal to this policy.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • On your iPhone: until you clear a day in the app or delete the app.
  • Backup: until you delete your backup account. In the app, open Stats, then Settings, then Delete backup account. This immediately deletes your account and all backed-up days from our server and ends Bare Days' access to your Sign in with Apple account. The days on your iPhone are not touched.
  • Sign out stops the backup but keeps your backup on our server, so you can sign in again later.

You can also ask us by email to delete your data or to send you a copy of it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and you can withdraw your consent to the backup at any time by deleting the backup account. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We process backup data because you asked us to provide the backup (performance of a service you requested).

Children

Bare Days is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
